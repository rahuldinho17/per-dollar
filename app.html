    Methodology. Cost per job = (input tokens × input price) + (output tokens × answer-length factor × output price). Length factors estimate how long each model's answers run for the same prompt — different tokenizers and response styles mean 1,000 tokens is not the same amount of answer everywhere; the live product measures these per task. Prices are standard-tier, first-party API list rates verified 3 August 2026, each linked to its source from the ledger; batch (~50% off) and cached-input (~90% off) rates are excluded here. Task definitions are usage-weighted from published studies (Anthropic Economic Index, OpenRouter State of AI, OpenAI usage report); each task states its assumptions above the ledger, and token footprints are estimates until the benchmark suite replaces them with measured medians. Capability. The CAPABILITY column is the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v4.1), tracked 3 Aug 2026 — an independent third-party score, not ours. VALUE is capability per euro of job cost, shown as a rating against the best option currently in view rather than a raw ratio — the underlying figure (capability points per $1) is on hover. Models with no published score show "no score" and are excluded when a capability floor is set; we never invent a number to fill a gap. The index is general intelligence, not your task: a model that clears the bar overall can still fail your specific workload, which is why every recommendation is validated on a sample before you switch. For custom tasks: 1 token ≈ ¾ of an English word; 100 lines of code ≈ 900 tokens. Monthly cost multiplies your stated volume by the cost of one job; default volumes are illustrative figures for a mid-size engineering org and should be replaced with your own. Note the scope: this prices the work sent to a model, not whether the work succeeded — a bug that takes three attempts costs three times as much here, and completion status lives in your issue tracker, not in token usage. Promotional prices are shown alongside standard list prices, never in place of them; rankings use the effective (promo) price by default because that is what this month's invoice will say, and the "plan with standard prices" toggle re-ranks everything at list price for budgeting and commitments. A promo ending is recorded as a promo end, not a price hike.
