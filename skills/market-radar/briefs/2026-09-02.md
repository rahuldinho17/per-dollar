# PerDollar Market Radar — Week of 2 Sep 2026

## 1. This week in one line

Two claims currently on the site are now wrong: the frontier price index has *risen* from 12 to 16, and the Gemini Flash prices we publish as standard are introductory rates with a scheduled doubling.

## 2. What happened

### Signal

- **The frontier index went up.** BenchLM's index reads **16 as of 2 September, 84% below its March 2023 base** — it was 12 and 88% a fortnight ago. Median flagship is $6.00 blended across 21 constituents. **Why it matters:** our methodology text says "frontier prices sit about 88% below their 2023 base." That is now a stale number pointing the wrong way, on a page whose whole claim is freshness. (high)

- **Gemini Flash pricing is introductory, with a doubling scheduled.** Google's own documentation reportedly puts Gemini 3.6, 3.7 and 3.8 Flash on **the same introductory pricing window through year end**, and a September dispatch lists "Gemini doubling" among the quarter's price changes. **Why it matters:** we publish $0.75/$3.75 for 3.6 and 3.7 as *standard*, verified 25 August. We have a promo mechanism built precisely for this and did not apply it. If the doubling lands, anyone who budgeted from our numbers is out by 2×. (high)

- **Three models launched that we do not carry.** Gemini 3.8 Flash (2 Sep, $0.75/1M in), Claude Fable 5.1 (GA 1 Sep, same $10/$50, cache reads down to $0.25), GLM-5.3 and GLM-5.3 Flash (19 and 27 Aug, $0.15/$0.50). **Why it matters:** GLM is the awkward one — we retired GLM-5.2 last week as "not found on their page," and the reason it was missing is that it had been superseded. Retiring was right; not noticing the successor was not. (high)

- **The category's own language has shifted.** CloudZero: *"The rates you budgeted in August are wrong in September."* A widely-cited dispatch opens with *"LLM pricing stopped being a rate card this quarter and became a moving target."* **Why it matters:** that is PerDollar's thesis, now stated by others as consensus. Being right is no longer differentiating; being *verifiable* is. (medium-high)

- **The cheap tier keeps climbing.** Median closed-small launch price: $0.53 in 2024, $3.00 across the first eight months of 2026. Mid-tier fell 38% over the same period. **Why it matters:** confirms the two-directional narrative we added in W33, and it is now well enough sourced to state as fact rather than observation. (medium)
