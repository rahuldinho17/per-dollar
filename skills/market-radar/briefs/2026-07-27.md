## 2. What happened

### Signal

- **Ramp opened public access to a free LLM Router with a paid spend dashboard behind it.** An OpenAI-compatible proxy that routes each request to the cheapest model meeting a quality bar, cutting costs ~30% by their numbers; the money is in the companion "AI Token Spend Management" dashboard for finance teams. *Why it matters:* this is the closest thing yet to your product, from a $44B company with three years of routing data across 70,000 customers. You cannot win on data breadth or horizontal spend management. (high)

- **The whole FinOps-for-AI field is converging on your buyer, fast.** CloudZero, Vantage, Finout, Amnic, Datadog, Harness all now ingest OpenAI/Anthropic usage as first-class providers; CloudZero explicitly targets the $500K+/month finance buyer with unit-economics attribution. *Why it matters:* "AI cost visibility" as a generic pitch is already crowded and well-funded. Your defensibility is not visibility — it's commerce-specific task cost and verified price history, which none of these have. (high)

- **The category's own framing has shifted from "sector" to "control point."** Investors are underwriting whoever becomes the system of record for a specific workflow, not "AI cost" broadly. *Why it matters:* directly prescribes your positioning — name the exact workflow (commerce AI spend) you intend to own, rather than being another cost tool. (medium-high)

- **Model churn continued** — Kimi K3, GLM-5.2, Gemini 3.6 Flash and others surfaced in coverage this week. *Why it matters:* validates the daily-refresh + discovery work just shipped; the market moves weekly, which is exactly the property that makes a live feed defensible against a static competitor table. (medium)
