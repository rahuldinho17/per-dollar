The problem

Model choice is made a thousand times a day. Nobody is watching.

Every engineer picks the biggest model because it is right there and the cost is somebody else's line item. A policy telling them otherwise is a nag nobody enforces. The decision is too frequent and too small to govern by hand — so it has to be answered where it is made.

01

Governance by policy does not work

Asking engineers to justify a model per task is a tax on everyone. The choice has to be made for them, at the moment of use, or it will not be made at all.

02

Set once, never revisited

Most teams picked a model at launch. New models land every couple of days and prices move underneath you — the default stopped being the right answer months ago.

03

Compliance beats cost, and nobody prices it

For a European customer, "must stay in the EU" is binding before price is even discussed. The EU-resident hosts that satisfy it appear in no pricing aggregator.

What it does

Three questions, answered where the work happens.

Your agent asks before it starts and reports when it finishes. No proxy sits in the request path, so nothing slows down and no prompt leaves your infrastructure.

01Decide — with jurisdiction first

The cheapest model capable enough for this task, from the models you can reach. Data residency is a hard filter applied before cost, so a Germany-only team is routed to German infrastructure rather than told to be careful.

02Stay inside a budget

Give it $500 per developer per month and it returns the most capable model that fits, not the cheapest. As the month's spend accumulates the ceiling falls and the pick downgrades on its own — no alert to action, no throttle to configure.

03Prove what it saved

Every routed task records what it cost and what the model your engineer would have reached for would have cost. The saving is an auditable number from your own traffic — no A/B test, no eval harness, no vendor claim.

04On prices you can check

Every price links to the provider's own page and says who confirmed it and when. Where we cannot source a number we say so rather than publish it. Corrections to our own errors are published as corrections.
