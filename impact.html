How these numbers are computed. Loop velocity is the median days between the date a signal was first heard (a logged field note, or the market-radar brief that surfaced it) and the date the resulting change shipped. Hit rate is worked ÷ graded — items still marked too-early are excluded rather than counted as wins, so the rate stays honest and will look worse before it looks better. A change counts as loop-driven only when a specific input can be named; anything else is counted as founder-initiated. Grades are assigned at the monthly review against the metric recorded at ship time, never retrofitted to match the outcome. What is deliberately not here: revenue. With no paying customer yet, any ROI figure in euros would be invented. These are the leading indicators that precede it — input volume, cycle time, and hit rate — and revenue joins this page the day a first audit is paid.
